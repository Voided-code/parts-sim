Parts Sim

Open-source structural, thermal and airflow simulation for your CAD parts.

Open a SolidWorks, STEP, IGES, STL or OBJ part, click the faces you want to hold and push, and see where it bends most and where it breaks first. The same setup runs linear and nonlinear static, frequency, buckling, fatigue, drop test, linear dynamic and optimization studies, plus steady and transient heat transfer. You can also put the part in a virtual wind tunnel and turn the aerodynamic pressure into a structural load. Everything runs on your machine; nothing is uploaded.

Try it in your browser Download

The browser version needs a modern browser (Chrome or Edge 113 or newer, Safari 26 or newer) for the GPU path. Without WebGPU it still opens and uses the CPU, with a smaller airflow grid.

What it does

  • Opens SolidWorks parts and assemblies without SolidWorks, plus STEP, IGES, BREP, STL, OBJ, 3MF, PLY and GLB.
  • Structural studies: linear and nonlinear static, frequency, buckling, fatigue, drop test, linear dynamic, topology and material optimization, with SolidWorks-style colour plots.
  • Break test: progressive damage that shows where a crack starts and the load it takes to grow.
  • Thermal: fixed temperatures, heat inputs and convection; steady state or over time.
  • Airflow: a 3D lattice-Boltzmann wind tunnel with drag, lift, Cd and frontal area, and a one-click wind load for the bend test.
  • Runs on the GPU through WebGPU where there is one, and falls back to the CPU.
  • Seven sample parts with ready-made setups, from a beam to the Ahmed car body.
  • Private: no account, no upload, no analytics. This site has no trackers and loads nothing from other sites.

Honest limits

  • Parts Sim is a design-comparison and learning tool, not a certified analysis.
  • Stresses at fixture edges and point loads are singular, as in any FEA.
  • The airflow grid is uniform and holds a few tens of millions of cells at most, so thin boundary layers are modelled, not resolved.
  • SolidWorks files are read from their cached display mesh, not the exact Parasolid geometry. Files from SolidWorks 2014 or older do not open.
  • The downloads are not code-signed yet, so the first launch needs one extra click (details).
